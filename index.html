Signature offer

Location opportunity assessment

Define the service line, geography and patient segment. Map the catchment and competitors. Gather demographic, access, growth and payer indicators. Assess referral sources and staffing constraints. Score, test the assumptions, and present findings with the further diligence required.

CriterionExample weightEvidence
Population and growth25%ACS and local projections
Service access indicators25%Provider locations and access measures
Competition20%Provider inventory and validation
Payer and economics15%Public indicators and client assumptions
Operational feasibility15%Staffing, access and site constraints

Illustrative weights. Each criterion is scored 1 to 5 with documented rules and sensitivity tests, and weights are agreed with the client.

Sources

Built on public data, with dates and limits stated.

Census American Community Survey and population estimates, HRSA data and shortage designations, CMS provider datasets, the NPPES registry, local planning and permitting records, state health data, and client-approved reimbursement, capacity and cost assumptions.

Public provider listings do not establish actual availability or demand. Financial feasibility needs volume, reimbursement, staffing, occupancy, startup and operating costs, and qualified partners where needed.

Oluchi Nwaobia, MPH, PMP, CSM

She is a public health–trained strategist and certified project management professional with approximately 13 years of experience turning complex requirements into working operations across healthcare, biotechnology, pharmacy, technology, energy, public health, academia and research.

Her work sits where strategy, data and delivery meet. She has managed a team of 25 analysts, contributed to a Lean Six Sigma initiative that reduced operational expenses by 45%, and supported enterprise system integrations from requirements through release, including data dictionaries, API and interface coordination, release management and transitions from legacy systems.

She has supported local, state and federal government clients for more than seven years, including more than five years at a large government contractor, where she authors standard operating procedures and federal technical documentation and manages delivery for complex, multi-stakeholder programs. Her analytical work spans healthcare claims operations, accounting ledger development, demographic analysis, clinical research needs assessments and executive performance reporting.
